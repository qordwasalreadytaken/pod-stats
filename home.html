About This Data

This analysis includes available data from all ranked ladder characters, including:

  • The top 1,000 overall ladder characters
  • The top 200 characters from each class ladder
  • Equipment usage and skill point allocations
  • Mercenary equipment and details

Character data and associated pages are updated regularly to reflect current ladder standings and trends.
