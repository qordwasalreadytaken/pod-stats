Character & Mercenary Item Search

Find characters and mercenaries using specific items

Search through both character equipment and mercenary equipment. Results will be color-coded:
Green: Character Equipment | Brown: Mercenary Equipment

Basic: type an item name (e.g. shako, enigma). Use and / or between terms (e.g. enigma and shako, shako or griff).
Sockets: with matches what is in the sockets (e.g. shield with skull, griff with jewel).
Negative sockets: use !with or without to exclude socket contents (e.g. shako !with rune, shield without rune or jewel).
Combined: earlier terms are extra requirements on the same character (e.g. enigma and shako !with topaz, glad and shield with skull).
Multiple clauses: separate full queries with ; to require all on the same character (e.g. griff with jewel; shield with jewel).
